Trackify Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy covers Trackify, the web system used by dispatch and office staff, and Trackify Driver, the mobile application used by employed drivers. Trackify is operated by AstreaBlue Intelligence Inc., Makati, Philippines, on behalf of client trucking companies (the “operator”). Accounts are issued by an operator to its employees. There is no public sign-up.

Information we collect

For account holders, Trackify records:

  • name, email address, role, company and branch affiliation;
  • acceptance of the Terms of Service and this policy, including date, IP address and browser information;
  • sign-in attempts and an audit trail of relevant business and administrative actions.

For drivers, Trackify also records employee, licence, contact and emergency-contact details; precise latitude, longitude, speed, heading and location accuracy while an eligible trip is being tracked; and optional profile photographs, proof-of-delivery photographs and expense receipts.

Location collection

Location is recorded only while a trip is released or in transit and only after the driver turns on “Share my location.” In the Android application, collection can continue in the background and while the phone is locked. A persistent notification tells the driver that their position is being shared. The driver can stop sharing at any time; recording also stops when delivery is confirmed or the trip is no longer eligible. Trackify does not record location between trips, off shift or on rest days.

Why we use the information

  • to show the operator’s dispatch team where a vehicle carrying a load is;
  • to provide estimated arrival information and establish a delivery record;
  • to operate accounts securely, process business records and meet legal obligations.

Location is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to rank drivers outside the trip record.

Who receives it

Information is available to the employing operator’s authorized dispatch and administrative staff, to hosting providers working under confidentiality obligations, and to government authorities when legally required. Addresses entered for routes may be sent to the public OpenStreetMap Nominatim service for geocoding; no driver identity is sent with the address. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Account records are kept while the account is active and afterwards for the legally required period. Trip records and their supporting accounting evidence are retained for ten years. The minute-by-minute location trail is retained for twelve months and then deleted automatically.

Security

Passwords and driver PINs are stored as hashes. Access is restricted by role, company and branch. Repeated failed sign-ins are throttled, important actions are audited, and traffic is encrypted in transit. Trackify does not currently offer two-factor authentication.

Your rights

Under Republic Act No. 10173, you may ask to be informed about, access, correct, object to, erase or block personal data held about you, request portability, and complain to the National Privacy Commission. Some records cannot be erased while tax or accounting law requires their retention; where this applies, we will explain why.

Contact

To exercise your rights or ask a privacy question, contact the Data Protection Officer, AstreaBlue Intelligence Inc., Makati, Philippines at admin@astreablue.com.

Changes

Material changes are published with a new version and effective date. Users are asked to accept revised terms and privacy information inside Trackify before continuing.
